# Supermart Grocery Sales Dashboard

## Project Overview

The Supermart Grocery Sales Dashboard is an interactive Excel dashboard developed to analyze supermarket sales data and understand overall business performance.

The dashboard helps management monitor sales, profit, product performance, discounts, and geographic performance through interactive charts, KPIs, and slicers.

The project focuses on converting raw sales data into meaningful business insights that support better decision-making.

## Business Objectives

The dashboard addresses the following business questions:

### 1. Overall Sales Performance
- What are the total sales and total profit?
- Which product categories contribute the largest share of total sales?
- How do sales change over time?
- Which regions generate the highest sales?

### 2. Product Performance
- Which categories and subcategories generate the highest sales?
- Which products have high sales but comparatively low profit?

### 3. Profit and Discount Analysis
- Which categories and regions generate the highest profit?
- Is there a relationship between discount and profit?

### 4. Geographic Performance
- Which states and cities generate the highest sales?
- Which locations have high sales but comparatively low profit?

## Dashboard Features

- Total Sales KPI
- Total Profit KPI
- Category-wise Sales Analysis
- Monthly Sales Trend
- Sales by Region
- Sales by State
- Top 10 Cities by Sales
- Profit by Category
- Profit by Region
- Discount vs Profit Analysis
- Interactive slicers for Order Date, Region, Category, and State

## Key Insights

The dashboard provides insights into:

- Overall sales and profit performance.
- Categories and subcategories contributing the most sales.
- Monthly sales trends.
- Regional and state-wise business performance.
